Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what data Spendly collects when you use our personal expense-tracking service ("Service"), how we use it, where it is stored, and the choices you have over it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the data needed to operate Spendly:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you record for your own expenses.
  • Technical data — basic session information (such as your login state) and, if enabled, minimal server logs containing IP address and request timestamps for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment information, precise location data, contacts, or any data from third-party financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to:

  • authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • store and display the expenses you record;
  • generate the category breakdowns and summaries shown in the app;
  • diagnose errors and prevent abuse of the Service.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train machine-learning models.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a SQLite database on the server that hosts Spendly. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never accessible to us in plain text. Data is retained for as long as your account remains active; when you delete your account, your personal data and expenses are removed from the primary database, and any residual copies in short-term backups age out on the normal backup rotation.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly keeps its dependency on third parties intentionally minimal. The Service currently relies on:

  • Google Fonts — used to load the DM Serif Display and DM Sans typefaces. Your browser fetches these fonts directly from Google's servers when you visit the site.

We do not embed advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or social-media widgets. If we introduce additional third-party services in the future, this policy will be updated before those services go live.

5. Cookies and Sessions

Spendly uses a single session cookie to keep you signed in between page loads. This cookie is essential for the Service to function and is removed when you log out or when your browser session ends.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data through your profile;
  • export your expense data in a machine-readable format;
  • delete your account and the personal data associated with it.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not directed to children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
